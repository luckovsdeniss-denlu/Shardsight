# Roadmap (everything except multiplayer)

All planned versions are done as of 0.14. What is missing from each is listed in CHANGELOG.md.

Each version is built and checked before the next starts.

- **0.8 Persistence and menu:** save/load, pause menu, fixed signing key support.
- **0.9 Progression:** stats that rise with use (225 cap), skills screen with up/down/locked
  arrows, Healing + Anatomy + bandages, Parrying + shields.
- **0.10 Reputation and PvP:** notoriety (blue/grey/red), murder counts and decay, criminal and
  aggressor flags, guards, bounties, ghosts and resurrection, safe and open-PvP zones.
- **0.11 Crafting and gathering:** Mining, Lumberjacking, smelting, Blacksmithy, Tailoring.
- **0.12 Housing and banking:** bank box, house deeds, secure storage, decay.
- **0.13 World:** dungeon, more monsters and towns, spell circles 5-8, Mark/Recall/Gate Travel,
  vendors, quests.
- **0.14 Polish:** sound, day/night, minimap, camera controls, character animation.

## Second roadmap (the rest, still no multiplayer)

- **0.15 Test and onboarding:** CI plays ~15 game-minutes of Auto mode and fails if the hero
  never fights, loots or improves, or if anyone ends up inside a wall; in-game help and tips.
- **0.16 Poison and potions:** poisoned monsters and weapons, the Cure spell, Anatomy 60 bandage
  cures, heal/cure/refresh potions (Alchemy).
- **0.17 Thieves:** Snooping, Stealing, Hiding, the Thieves' Guild, getting caught.
- **0.18 Animals:** Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Veterinary, pets that follow and fight.
- **0.19 Bards:** Musicianship, Peacemaking, Provocation, Enticement.
- **0.20 Society:** fame and karma titles, guilds (green / orange at war), a Felucca / Trammel
  ruleset choice.
- **0.21 Shops:** NPC vendors with supply and demand and per-town prices; player vendors.
- **0.22 Crafting and skills depth:** exceptional items and maker's marks, coloured-ore bonuses,
  tool wear; Archery, Fencing, Mace Fighting, Tracking, Camping, Arms Lore, Item ID, Begging,
  Spirit Speak.
- **0.23 Magic, travel and housing depth:** the rest of circle 8, scrolls and spellbooks,
  paralyze; a runebook; bigger houses, co-owners, lock-downs.
- **0.24 Art:** low-poly models and textures (CC0 packs).
- **0.25 Platform:** signed release build for Google Play, icon and splash, mesh batching.
